4. Official Company Bank Accounts & Secure Payment Policy

All payments must be made strictly to our official company bank account. We will never ask you to transfer funds to a personal account.

Official Payment Protection Clause: All monetary remittances—including holiday deposits, flight ticketing charges, visa processing fees, and hotel reservations—must be remitted exclusively to the verified corporate bank accounts of Star Plus Travel & Tourism LLC (in the UAE) or Star Plus Travel (PVT) LTD (in Sri Lanka), or completed via our authorized point-of-sale terminals or accredited installment gateways. Under no circumstances will any representative, employee, or agent of Star Plus Travels instruct a client to transfer funds into an individual or personal account.

Star Plus Travels provides visa application review, document compliance auditing, and authorized portal lodgment services. Crucial Notice: The final authority to grant, delay, or reject any tourist, transit, or entry visa rests exclusively with sovereign immigration bodies, including the UAE General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA), Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP), and relevant foreign embassies.

Non-Refundable Government Fees: Government processing fees, consular stamp duties, and administrative filing costs are non-refundable under any circumstances once an application is lodged into official immigration databases, regardless of whether a visa is approved, delayed, or rejected.

6. Cancellation, Amendments & Refunds

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to our official email address (info@starplustraveluae.com). Refund entitlements are determined according to the following schedule:

  • 30+ Days Prior to Departure: 90% refund of land package costs (airline tickets subject to carrier tariff rules).

  • 7 to 14 Days Prior to Departure: 50% refund of land package costs.
  • Under 7 Days or No-Show: Non-refundable due to committed hotel and transportation locks.

It is the traveler's sole responsibility to ensure their passport possesses a minimum of six (6) months validity from the scheduled return date. Star Plus Travels strongly advises all travelers to hold comprehensive international travel medical and luggage insurance. For Schengen visas and select international destinations, proof of insurance meeting statutory minimum medical coverage (EUR 30,000) is mandatory.
